# Axon vs EDC vs Atlan

## Axon Data Governance
- Business-focused
- Glossary, policies, ownership
- Used by stewards and business users

## Enterprise Data Catalog (EDC)
- Technical metadata management
- Lineage and impact analysis
- Used by architects and engineers

## Atlan
- Unified data catalog
- Strong collaboration and UI
- Limited native ETL governance enforcement

## Key Difference
Axon + EDC together provide stronger enterprise-grade governance when paired with Informatica IDMC.
